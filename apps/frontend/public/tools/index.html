Free trading calculators

Three calculators that do the arithmetic every risk plan needs. No account, no email, nothing stored — the maths runs in your browser.

Position Size Calculator

How many units to buy so a stop-loss hit costs exactly what you decided in advance. Takes account balance, risk percentage, entry and stop; returns size, amount at risk and the leverage your inputs imply.

Risk / Reward Calculator

Entry, stop and target in — ratio out, plus the win rate you'd need just to break even at that ratio. The second number is the one that decides whether the first one means anything.

Breakeven Win Rate Calculator

How often you have to be right at a given risk/reward to end up flat, including spread, fees and slippage — the drag most calculators leave out. Also computes expectancy in R at your actual win rate.

Why we built these

Position sizing and expectancy are the two places retail traders lose money for reasons that have nothing to do with reading a chart. They're arithmetic — solvable, unglamorous, and skipped constantly.

We sell a chart-analysis tool, so the honest framing is this: these calculators tell you how much to risk. They have no opinion on whether the trade is worth taking. That second question is what our product is for, and you can answer it without us — but you can't skip the arithmetic on this page either way.

These numbers tell you how much to risk. They can't tell you whether the setup is worth risking anything on — that's a different question, and it's the one we built Tickrify for: paste a chart, get entry, stop, targets and risk, or an honest "no trade". Three free trade plans, no card.
