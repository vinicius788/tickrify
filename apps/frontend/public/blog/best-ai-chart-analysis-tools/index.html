The four things that actually matter

After backtesting our own engine against a year of market data, we're convinced the quality gap between tools comes down to four questions:

1. Live data: does the tool anchor its read in real, current market data — or is it guessing price levels from pixels?
2. Commitment: does it force a structured verdict (direction, entry, stop, targets, invalidation) or hedge with "could go either way"?
3. Honest confidence: is the confidence number calibrated against measured outcomes, or decoration?
4. Willingness to say WAIT: does it protect you from bad setups, or does it produce a "signal" for every chart to feel useful?

How to choose (a 5-minute test)

Take the same chart screenshot and run it through any two tools plus ChatGPT. Then check: Did the levels match the real current price? Did the tool commit to a verdict with invalidation, or hedge? Ask what its confidence number means and how it was measured. And watch whether it's willing to tell you there's no trade. The tool that survives those four checks — at a price you'd pay monthly without flinching — is your answer. For most traders that's $0 (ChatGPT) or under ~$20/mo; anything above that should be buying you journaling, backtesting, or community, not just chart reads.
