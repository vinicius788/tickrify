How Tickrify Works, in 5 Short Demos

You send a screenshot or a photo of any chart, and you get back a structured read: a direction (BUY, SELL or WAIT), a confidence score, and for a trade, the entry, stop loss, two targets and the risk-reward. Demo 01 · 26 s

From chart to structure

A raw chart is mostly noise. Tickrify checks the pieces a trader would check (market structure, liquidity, fair value gaps, order blocks, momentum and risk) and combines them into one confidence score. The result is a direction and a reason for it, not a guess dressed up as certainty.

Demo 02 · 27 s

The workflow: from setup to tracked result

The analysis is where the work starts, not where it ends:

  • Get the setup. Entry, stop, TP1 and TP2, drawn on your chart.
  • Mark it. Tell Tickrify if you are taking the trade or passing.
  • Log the result. In My Trades, record how it ended and your real P&L.
  • Track performance. The trading journal shows every analysis and every result, day by day.

That record is what turns a one-off opinion into a process you can review.

Demo 03 · 22 s

Not every chart deserves a trade

This is the one to watch if you got a WAIT and wondered whether it worked. It did. Each chart goes through a decision checklist, and most charts, most of the time, do not pass it: no clean setup, or a risk-reward that is not worth taking. WAIT means there is no clean entry right now, and the read tells you what would have to change for a trade to set up.

The goal is not more trades. It is fewer, better ones. That is also why WAIT results do not use up your free signals.

Demo 04 · 20 s

Multi-timeframe analysis

One chart does not tell the whole story. Send your entry chart plus up to two higher timeframes (for example 15M, 1H and 4H) and Tickrify reads them as one market: the higher timeframe gives the context, the middle one the direction, and the lower one the entry. You get a single analysis that says how many timeframes agree.

Multi-timeframe analysis is part of the paid plans, together with the deep analysis mode.

Demo 05 · 19 s

Analyze a chart from your phone

No app to install. Open tickrify.com in your phone browser, take a photo of the screen with the chart or pick a screenshot, and you get the same analysis you get on desktop.

One tip for photos: keep the chart straight, sharp, without glare and fully in the frame. If a photo is blurry or cropped, Tickrify says so and lowers its confidence instead of guessing, and an unreadable image is rejected.

The videos use example data. Nothing shown is a trade signal.
