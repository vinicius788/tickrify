## Os 6 pilares (rotação semanal)
1. **Live Analysis** — cola um gráfico real na Tickrify, mostra o resultado na tela (o produto é o vídeo)
2. **WAIT Protection** — setups tentadores em que a IA disse WAIT (o diferencial anti-sinal)
3. **Trading Psychology** — overtrading, revenge trade, tilt (maior alcance do nicho)
4. **Honest AI** — "o que a IA NÃO consegue fazer" (constrói a marca anti-hype)
5. **Pattern em 15s** — mini-educação visual (order block, FVG, liquidez)
6. **Challenge/Prova** — "IA vs este gráfico", resultado no fim (retenção alta)

**Estrutura padrão de cada short:** HOOK (0–2s, texto grande na tela) → PAYOFF (3–20s, tela do produto/gráfico com zoom) → CTA (últimos 3s: "3 free analyses — link in bio").

## Semana 1 — Apresentação do conceito

**D1 · Live Analysis** — Hook: `I pasted my chart into an AI. Watch.`
Beats: print do BTC no TradingView → colar na Tickrify → zoom no verdict + entry/stop/targets aparecendo → freeze no chip Read/Signal. CTA padrão.
Caption: The AI reads entry, stop, targets and risk in seconds. A second opinion before you click buy. #trading #ai #daytrading

**D2 · Psychology** — Hook: `Your worst trades happen in these 10 seconds.`
Beats: timer de 10s na tela → texto: "see setup → FOMO → click buy" → corta pra Tickrify analisando calmamente → "add a checkpoint between impulse and entry."
Caption: The 10 seconds between seeing a setup and clicking buy are where accounts die.

**D3 · WAIT Protection** — Hook: `This setup looks PERFECT. The AI said no.`
Beats: gráfico bonito de breakout → colar na Tickrify → verdict WAIT + motivo na tela → texto: "the trades you skip are free wins."
Caption: A tool with no signal quota is free to tell you the truth: there's no trade here.

**D4 · Honest AI** — Hook: `AI trading tools are lying to you. (I built one.)`
Beats: prints fake de "92% win rate" riscados com X vermelho → texto: "no chart AI has a proven edge" → "so we built a second opinion, not a signal seller."
Caption: Honest take from inside the industry: if they promise profits, run.

**D5 · Pattern 15s** — Hook: `Order Block in 15 seconds.`
Beats: gráfico limpo → zoom no OB com anotação → 3 bullets na tela: origin / mitigation / reaction → Tickrify marcando OB num gráfico real.
Caption: The 15-second version of order blocks. Save this.

**D6 · Challenge** — Hook: `AI vs this chart. Guess the verdict.`
Beats: mostra gráfico 2s → enquete mental "BUY / SELL / WAIT?" → revela análise da Tickrify → resultado.
Caption: Comment your read BEFORE watching the verdict. Be honest.

**D7 · Live Analysis** — Hook: `Gold chart. Live AI read. No edit.`
Beats: XAUUSD ao vivo → colar → mostrar leitura completa em tempo real (sem cortes = credibilidade).
Caption: One take, no edits. This is what the read looks like.
